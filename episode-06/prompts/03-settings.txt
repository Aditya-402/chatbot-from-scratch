Task : Add a menu bar to the window with a Settings menu, with the same settings as Google AI Studio, so I can change how the bot picks its words

Context :
- Use the virtual environment .venv in this folder. Don't create a new one.
- In bot.py, let ask() also take the settings: temperature, top_p and max_output_tokens. Keep the model and the thinking setting as they are.
- Add a menu bar at the top of the window with one menu, Settings.
- Settings > Temperature... opens a small dialog with a slider from 0 to 2, in steps of 0.1, starting at 1.0.
- Settings > Top-P... opens a small dialog with a slider from 0 to 1, in steps of 0.05, starting at 0.95.
- Settings > Max tokens... opens a small dialog with a slider from 10 to 2000, in steps of 10, starting at 2000.
- Each dialog shows the slider's current value, an OK button and a Cancel button.
- Settings > Top-K: 64 (fixed for this model) is shown greyed out, and top_k is left out of the call.
- Settings > Reset to defaults puts every setting back where it started.
- Under the menu bar, one small grey line always shows the current settings, for example: Temperature 1.0 · Top-P 0.95 · Max tokens 2000.
- The grey line under each reply also shows the settings used, and "stopped: max tokens" when the answer was cut short.
- Keep everything else in the window as it is: the chat, the token counter, the error messages and the colours.
- The terminal chat in bot.py keeps working with the starting settings.

Folder arch :
- ui.py : the window (frontend)
